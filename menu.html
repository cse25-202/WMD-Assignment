Wildlife Informational Page

DIET

Lions are apex predators and carnivores. They primarily hunt large ungulates such as zebra, wildebeest, and buffalo to sustain the pride.[3]

HABITAT

They thrive in grasslands, savannas, and open woodlands. They prefer areas with enough cover for stalking and proximity to water sources.[3]

BEHAVIOURS

Social Behaviour

Lions are the only truly social cats, living in prides consisting of related females, their offspring, and a few resident males.[3]

Communication

Prides use roaring to signal territory boundaries, along with scent marking and complex body language to maintain social bonds.[3]

Activity Patterns

Lions are crepuscular, meaning they are most active during the cooler hours of dawn and dusk, often resting for 20 hours a day.[3]
