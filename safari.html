Safari Planning & Preparation

Prepare for your journey with practical insights on logistics and safety.

Best Time to Visit Botswana

Dry Season (Peak Season)

Months
May to October
Temperature
Weather Cool and dry 6-25°C
Advantages
Peak wildlife viewing as animals gather at water sources; water-based activities like mokoro are in full swing.
Considerations
Highest prices of the year; very cold morning game drives; requires booking 9–12 months in advance.

Green Season (Wet Season)

Months
November to April
Temperature
Hot and humid (average 22°C – 34°C) with spectacular thunderstorms.
Advantages
Lush green landscapes; calving season for newborns; migratory birdlife; lower off-peak rates.
Considerations
Some roads may be impassable; high mosquito activity; limited water-based activities due to low delta levels.

Safari Packing List

Essential Documents

  • Valid Passport and necessary Visas
  • Travel Insurance documentation
  • Vaccination cards and health certificates

Clothing

  • Neutral, earthy tones (khaki, olive, sand) to blend in
  • Lightweight, breathable fabrics (cotton or linen)
  • Layers for the cold: fleece, windbreaker, and warm beanie
  • Sturdy closed-toe hiking shoes with ankle support

Equipment

  • High-quality binoculars and camera with extra memory cards
  • Flashlight or headlamp for campsite navigation
  • Portable power bank for charging electronics
  • Reusable water bottle

Sun and Bug Protection

  • High SPF 50+ sunscreen and lip balm
  • Insect repellent containing DEET
  • Wide-brimmed hat and polarized sunglasses
  • Malaria prophylaxis (as recommended by your doctor)
Safari Safety Rules
Vehicle Safety
  • Always stay inside the vehicle unless your guide says it's safe
  • Never stand up or lean out while animals are nearby
  • Keep noise to a minimum to avoid startling wildlife
  • Follow your guide's instructions immediately and without question
  • No sudden movements that might provoke animals
Camp Safety
  • Never walk alone after dark - always request an escort/li>
  • Keep tent zipped at all times to prevent animals entering
  • Don't leave food or scented items in your tent
  • Use provided walkways and stay within designated areas
  • Keep a flashlight with you at all times
  • Never approach animals, even if they seem tame
Wildlife Encounters
  • Maintain safe distance - never try to touch or feed animals
  • No flash photography (disturbs and may provoke animals)
  • Stay calm if an animal approaches - let it pass
  • Never run from an animal/li>
  • Be especially cautious around mothers with young
  • Buffalo and hippos are extremely dangerous - give wide berth
Photography Etiquette
  • Respect other guests - don't block views or make excessive noise
  • No flash photography (disturbs wildlife and ruins others' photos)
  • Patience is key - wait for the right moment
  • Don't ask guides to get closer or provoke animals for shots
  • Share rhino sightings responsibly - never post locations publicly
