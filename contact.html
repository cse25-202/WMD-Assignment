Frequently Asked Questions

Is this an official tourism website?

No, this is an educational student project designed to provide information about Botswana's Big Five wildlife. For official tourism information, please visit Botswana Tourism Organisation's website.

Can you help me book a safari?

This portal provides educational information only. Please contact licensed safari operators in Botswana for booking assistance.

How can I support wildlife conservation?

Choose ethical safari operators, never buy wildlife products, respect viewing guidelines, and consider donating to reputable conservation organizations working in Botswana.

Where does the information come from?

Information is compiled from wildlife research, conservation organizations, and educational resources about African wildlife and Botswana's ecosystems.
